# Video Tutorials

Recorded walkthroughs of RustGIS, oldest first: start at the top for a guided tour, or jump to
the one that matches your task. Every video is part of the
[RustGIS playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLBQuwPke8dNI) on the Open Geospatial
Solutions channel; subscribe there to catch new ones.

For written, step-by-step versions of the same workflows, see
[Getting Started](getting-started.md) and the [User Guide](user-guide/index.md).

[Watch the full playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLBQuwPke8dNI){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

**38:57 · November 2026 · [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/XFhqlSiKn6U)**

The full introduction, and the best place to start. Covers what RustGIS is and why it was
built, then tours its main features: loading data, symbology, the attribute table,
geoprocessing with 1,000+ GeoLibre/Whitebox tools, rasters, the 3D globe, web basemaps and the
AI Assistant.
